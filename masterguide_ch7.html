Advanced Threats & Social Engineering

Sophisticated attacks jo months tak undetected reh sakte hain — human psychology exploit karna

🕵️
APT
Advanced Persistent Threat — stealth, long-term attack
Advanced▾
What is it

Long-term targeted attack — attacker mahino ya saalo tak network mein chupa rehta hai bina detect hue. Nation-states typically. Goal: data exfiltration ya sabotage — not ransomware quick hit.

Characteristics

Advanced: Zero-days, custom malware, multiple vectors
Persistent: Months/years long campaign
Threat: Specific high-value target

📰 Real World

SolarWinds (2020): Russia's SVR ne SolarWinds software update mein malware inject kiya (supply chain). 18,000 organizations ne infected update install kiya. US government agencies breach — 9 months undetected.

🔭
Cyber Espionage
Digital jasoos — state/corporate secrets chori
Advanced▾
What is it

Government ya corporations ke sensitive information chori karna digital means se. Military plans, technology secrets, diplomatic cables, trade secrets — sab target. Traditional spy agencies ka cyber equivalent.

Players

China (APT40, APT10), Russia (APT28 Fancy Bear, APT29 Cozy Bear), North Korea (Lazarus Group), Iran. India bhi increasingly targeted.

⚔️
Cyber Warfare
Nations ke beech digital war — critical infrastructure target
Advanced▾
What is it

State-sponsored cyberattacks dusre nations ko disrupt ya damage karne ke liye. Power grids, water systems, financial systems, military systems target hote hain. 5th domain of warfare — land, sea, air, space, cyber.

Examples

Ukraine power grid attacks by Russia (2015, 2016). Estonia DDoS (2007). Stuxnet vs Iran. NotPetya Ukraine attack ($10B+ global damage). These are acts of war.

📦
Supply Chain Attack
Third-party vendor ke through attack — trusted software compromise
Advanced▾
What is it

Direct target attack mushkil hai. To attacker uske software supplier, hardware vendor, ya IT provider ko compromise karta hai. Target khud infected software install kar leta hai thinking it's legitimate.

Why devastating

Trusted source se aata hai — security bypasses. Scale: ek vendor compromise = hazaron customers compromise simultaneously. Defense very difficult.

📰 Real World

SolarWinds Orion (2020) + XZ Utils (2024): Open source library mein backdoor inject kiya gaya 2 years ke patient social engineering ke baad. Nearly compromised OpenSSH on millions of Linux servers.

🎭
Social Engineering
Human psychology hack karna — technical bypass nahi, human bypass
Advanced▾
What is it

Humans ko manipulate karke confidential info reveal karwana ya actions perform karwana. Technical skills ki zaroorat nahi — psychological manipulation. Phishing, pretexting, baiting, quid pro quo sab forms.

Techniques

Pretexting: Fake identity banao (IT support, bank)
Baiting: Infected USB chhodna parking mein
Quid Pro Quo: "IT help ke badle password do"
Tailgating: Physically follow someone through secure door

🧠 Analogy

"Tumhara PC virus infected hai, main IT se hun, abhi remote access do" — yahi social engineering. Firewall bypass nahi kiya, tumhe manipulate kiya khud access dene ke liye.

💪
Brute Force Attack
Try every combination — until it works
Advanced▾
What is it

Systematically har possible password/key combination try karna jab tak sahi na mile. Simple 4-digit PIN = 10,000 combinations. "abc123" jaise common passwords seconds mein crack.

Types

Simple Brute Force: aaa, aab, aac...
Dictionary Attack: Common words list
Rainbow Table: Pre-computed hash lookups
Hybrid: Dictionary + variations

🛡️ Defense
  • Rate limiting — 3 attempts failed → lockout
  • CAPTCHA
  • Long, complex passwords — "Tr0ub4dor&3" better than "password123"
  • MFA — even if password cracked, need 2nd factor
🗃️
Credential Stuffing
Leaked passwords ko doosre sites pe try karna
Advanced▾
What is it

Data breach se leaked username-password pairs ko automated tools se hazaron other sites pe try karna. Same password everywhere use karte ho? Ek site breach = sab sites compromise.

Scale

Billions of credentials dark web pe available. Tools like Snipr, STORM automate yeh process. 0.1% success rate bhi millions of accounts = huge impact.

🛡️ Defense
  • Unique password har site pe — password manager use karo (Bitwarden, 1Password)
  • haveibeenpwned.com pe check karo tumhara email leaked hai kya
  • MFA always
🏢
Insider Threat
Ghar ka bhed lanka dhaye — employee ya contractor
Advanced▾
Types

Malicious: Deliberately data steal ya damage kare (disgruntled employee)
Negligent: Accidentally security mistake kare (click phishing, lose laptop)
Compromised: External attacker ne account highjack kiya

Detection

UEBA (behavior analytics) — normal se deviation. Unusual access times, large data downloads, off-hours logins. DLP tools unusual data movement flag karte hain.

📰 Real World

Edward Snowden (2013): NSA contractor — 1.7 million classified documents copied aur media ko diye. Sab access legitimate tha — audit trail nahi tha ki woh itna sab ek saath download kar raha hai.

👦
Script Kiddie
Beginner attacker jo tools use karta hai bina samjhe
Advanced▾
What is it

Unskilled individuals jo publicly available tools/scripts use karte hain attacks ke liye bina understanding ke. "Point and click" hacking. Real hackers inhe seriously nahi lete, par inka sheer volume ek threat hai.

Still dangerous

Volume mein dangerous — hazaron script kiddies = significant DDoS. Basic vulnerabilities exploit kar lete hain. Patched systems ka importance inhi se pata chalta hai.

⚙️
Cyber-Physical Systems
Digital attacks with physical consequences
Advanced▾
What is it

Systems jahan digital aur physical world meet karte hain — industrial control systems (ICS), SCADA, power plants, water treatment, hospital equipment. Hack karo → physical world mein damage.

Danger

Power grid hack → lights off city mein. Water treatment hack → poison in water. Hospital device hack → patient harm. These are life-and-death scenarios — not just data theft.
