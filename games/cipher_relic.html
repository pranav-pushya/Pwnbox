💡 WHAT THIS TEACHES: CLASSICAL CIPHERS, POLYALPHABETIC ATTACKS & XOR

Monoalphabetic substitution ciphers (Caesar) preserve letter frequency distributions, making them vulnerable to single-minute frequency analysis. Polyalphabetic ciphers (Vigenère) disguise frequencies by applying multiple shifts across a key. In modern computing, symmetric cryptography (AES, ChaCha20, One-Time Pads) relies on the reversible mathematical purity of the XOR (⊕) logic gate, where (P ⊕ K) ⊕ K = P.
